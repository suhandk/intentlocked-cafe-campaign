Key/type checklist (use these exact discriminators, not alternative types):
CAFE / LOCATION / PRODUCT: text with value.
OFFER: percent_off (value, unit percent), amount_off (amount, currency INR),
or buy_get (buy, get), according to what the customer actually reads.
DATES: dates (absolute start/end, original_phrase, timezone Asia/Kolkata).
DAYS: days (weekdays MON/TUE/WED/THU/FRI/SAT/SUN); extract printed weekday names
even if the same line also states dates. Saturday / Sunday means SAT and SUN.
ELIGIBILITY: eligibility (scope all/students/custom, description).
ID_RULE: id_rule (required boolean) only if an ID requirement is explicitly stated.
TIME_WINDOW: time_window (start/end HH:MM) only if hours are explicitly stated.
DINE_IN_RULE: dine_in_rule (scope dine_in_only/takeaway_only/both) only if an
explicit dining/takeaway policy is stated. "Visit", "come", or "invited" is a CTA,
not evidence of a dine-in/takeaway restriction or permission.
MIN_PURCHASE_RULE: minimum_purchase (amount, currency INR), only if stated.
COMBINE_RULE: combine_rule (allowed boolean), only if combining offers is stated.

Audience targeting is different from eligibility. Student-focused language or
hashtags do not make an offer student-only. "Open to all" explicitly means scope
all. Only an explicit restriction such as "students only" means scope students.
Conflicting explicit policies are ambiguous. Do not infer unspecified conditions
from the kind of business, a visit invitation, or normal business practice.
If "20% off on coffee" is visible, PRODUCT is stated as coffee; it must not be
not_stated just because it shares evidence with OFFER. Evidence lines can support
multiple different keys. Finish by checking all twelve keys independently.

In particular, an invitation "All students are invited!" followed by "Open to all"
does not exclude anyone: eligibility is all, and its evidence must include the
explicit Open to all statement. "All students are invited" alone is audience
targeting, not a student-only policy. Do not extract students eligibility from
that invitation. Inspect later lines before choosing eligibility evidence.
